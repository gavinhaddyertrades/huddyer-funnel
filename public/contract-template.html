Section 2

Program Description

The Huddyer Trades Mentorship Program ("Program") is a private, high-level futures trading education and coaching experience led by Hudson Shaffer. The Program is designed to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of professional futures trading strategies, risk management principles, and the mindset required to operate consistently in live markets.

Program deliverables include, but are not limited to:

  • Live and recorded trading sessions and market breakdowns
  • Direct access to Hudson Shaffer for personalized coaching and strategy reviews
  • Private community access (Discord or equivalent platform)
  • Proprietary educational materials, frameworks, and playbooks
  • Ongoing curriculum updates for the duration of the enrollment period

The scope and structure of the Program may be updated at the Mentor's discretion to reflect current market conditions and best practices. Students will receive advance notice of any material changes.

Section 4

90-Day Results Guarantee

Our Commitment to Your Success

If the Student fully completes all assigned coursework, attends all scheduled coaching calls, actively participates in the community, and implements the strategies as taught — and does not see measurable progress in their trading within 90 days of their program start date — Huddyer Trades will continue to work with the Student at no additional charge until the agreed results are achieved, or provide a full refund at its sole discretion.

Eligibility for this guarantee requires the Student to:

  • Complete 100% of assigned modules and educational materials within the 90-day window
  • Attend no fewer than 80% of all scheduled live sessions and coaching calls
  • Submit all requested trading journals, homework, and accountability check-ins on time
  • Actively apply the strategies, risk management rules, and frameworks provided
  • Maintain consistent, documented trading activity throughout the Program

Students who do not satisfy all eligibility criteria above forfeit the right to invoke this guarantee. This guarantee is available only to first-time enrollments and may not be combined with any other offer or promotion.
